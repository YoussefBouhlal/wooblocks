# WooBlocks Storefront

The WooBlocks fashion storefront. This vocabulary distinguishes editorial presentation from the store's commercial records.

## Language

**Site identity**:
The merchant's configured site title and optional logo, representing their store rather than the theme.
_Avoid_: Theme identity, WooBlocks wordmark

**Theme identity**:
The WooBlocks name identifying the theme, distinct from the merchant's site identity.
_Avoid_: Store name, merchant branding

**Source storefront**:
The existing shopping experience used as the visual reference for WooBlocks.
_Avoid_: Parent theme

**Shipped defaults**:
The storefront appearance supplied by WooBlocks before a merchant customizes it, distinct from their saved design choices.
_Avoid_: Merchant styling, customized storefront

**Shipped template**:
A page layout supplied by WooBlocks before a merchant customizes it.
_Avoid_: Saved template, merchant layout

**Saved template**:
A merchant-saved page layout that overrides its shipped template.
_Avoid_: Shipped template, theme default

**Editorial section**:
A curated presentation of imagery and copy, such as a seasonal story or collection promotion. Its promotional claims do not establish actual prices or discounts.
_Avoid_: Discount rule, product record

**Catalog**:
The store's actual products, variations, prices, and availability.
_Avoid_: Demo collection

**Product category**:
A named grouping of products used for browsing the catalog.
_Avoid_: Page, editorial section

**Cart**:
The customer's current selection of products and quantities before an order is placed.
_Avoid_: Order

**Cart drawer**:
A compact view of the current cart that opens without leaving the current page.
_Avoid_: Checkout

**Checkout**:
The step where a customer supplies delivery and payment information and places an order.
_Avoid_: Cart

**Order confirmation**:
The outcome shown to the customer after an order is placed. It is not, by itself, proof that payment has settled.
_Avoid_: Payment receipt

**Customer account**:
A shopper's registered identity with the store and its associated orders, addresses, and account details, distinct from an administrator account or a guest cart.
_Avoid_: Administrator account, guest session

**Post discussion**:
The comments and replies associated with a blog post, distinct from reviews of a product.
_Avoid_: Product reviews

**Closed discussion**:
A post discussion that no longer accepts new comments or replies. Its existing comments remain part of the discussion.
_Avoid_: Deleted discussion, hidden discussion
